### Consensus method

The refreshed board counts **independent source families**, not articles. Two
NFL.com articles or two RotoBaller articles count as one vote from that family.
An analyst mention becomes draftable only after it is checked against the August
21 three-source market file, current depth chart, and injury context.

- **Tier A:** at least three independent recommendation families, plus a viable
  current price and role.
- **Tier B:** two independent recommendation families plus role or market
  corroboration.
- **Tier C / watch:** one family, material disagreement, or a role/health/price
  problem that prevents an ordinary draft recommendation.

The market file now blends ESPN PPR, the August 21 FantasyPros half-PPR consensus
from 103 experts, and FFToday's half-PPR ADP; 225 of 300 sheet rows match all three
sources. [Machine-readable market context](./market_context.json)

### Simple draft rule

Take a sleeper only when all three are true:

1. The player is still available at or after the target range.
2. The roster can absorb the player's role or injury uncertainty.
3. The pick offers more upside than the best available stable veteran.

Do not draft more than two role-dependent rookies unless the bench is unusually
deep. In short-bench leagues, favor early usability: Reed, Likely, Shaheed, Goff,
Kincaid, or Rodriguez before Lane, Ferguson, Douglas, or Washington.

## Best fits for this scoring

### 1. Chig Okonkwo — best late-TE value

**Why coverage is building:** FantasyPros' August 18 sleeper poll ranked Okonkwo
first among tight ends, CBS highlighted his guaranteed-money investment and path to
the vacated Zach Ertz role, and the Commanders' own preview said the team can use
him more as a receiving threat because John Bates handles primary blocking work.
[FantasyPros TE sleeper consensus](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/rankings/te-sleepers.php)
[CBS sleepers](https://secure-www.cbssports.com/fantasy/football/news/dave-richards-sleepers-1-0-mahomes-could-be-the-steal-of-your-draft/)
[Commanders TE preview](https://www.commanders.com/news/commanders-2026-training-camp-preview-tight-end)

**Why he fits:** The move from Tennessee to Jayden Daniels creates a better QB and
offensive environment. Okonkwo has recorded at least 50 receptions in three
straight seasons, while his YAC ability provides a path to yardage without needing
unusually high touchdown volume. That matters because this league awards only five
points per receiving TD.

**Failure case:** Ben Sinnott earns more routes than expected, Washington spreads
the vacated targets across several players, or Okonkwo remains a low-TD, 500-yard
receiver. Treat him as a late TE bet, not a guaranteed starter.

### 2. Jonah Coleman — best deep RB contingency bet

**Why coverage is building:** Coleman ranked first in FantasyPros' August 18 RB
sleeper poll and appeared on NFL.com's late-round rookie list. NFL.com described a
path to becoming the 1A if J.K. Dobbins misses time, while PFF's prospect work
identified receiving production, pass protection, and after-catch ability that can
support more than an early-down role.
[FantasyPros RB sleeper consensus](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/rankings/rb-sleepers.php/1000)
[NFL.com late-round rookies](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-fantasy-football-15-rookies-worth-a-late-round-draft-pick)
[PFF receiving-RB evaluation](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-2026-nfl-draft-top-3-receiving-running-back-prospects)

**Why he fits:** Receiving work survives negative scripts, and a three-down skill
set creates more ways to reach a 100-yard rushing or receiving milestone than a
touchdown-only handcuff. He is inexpensive enough that the uncertain Denver
committee is acceptable.

**Failure case:** Dobbins and RJ Harvey remain healthy and Coleman opens as a clear
third option. He is a bench stash, not a Week 1 starter.

### 3. Denzel Boston — best rookie WR role bet

**Why coverage is building:** NFL.com recommended Boston in Round 12 or later and
also labeled him an early-rising rookie. The Browns reported on August 11 that he
was taking most of his reps with the first-team offense after a camp featuring
contested and deep catches.
[NFL.com late sleepers](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-fantasy-football-six-late-round-sleepers-to-target)
[NFL.com rookie analysis](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-fantasy-football-15-rookies-worth-a-late-round-draft-pick)
[Browns first-team report](https://www.clevelandbrowns.com/news/denzel-boston-posts-standout-performance-on-day-12-training-camp-observations)

**Why he fits:** His perimeter/downfield profile can produce concentrated yardage,
100-yard games, and long-play bonuses. At ADP 141, the cost does not require him to
become Cleveland's immediate WR1.

**Failure case:** Cleveland's QB and offensive-line uncertainty suppresses the
entire passing game, or KC Concepcion and Jerry Jeudy dominate targets. Boston is a
high-variance bench receiver.

### 4. Rashid Shaheed — best explosive-play fit

**Why coverage is building:** Yahoo's sleeper roundtable and PFF's sleeper analysis
have highlighted Shaheed. Seattle's own July preview said the plan was for a larger
offensive role after a full offseason with Sam Darnold.
[Yahoo sleeper roundtable](https://ca.sports.yahoo.com/news/10-sleepers-you-cannot-sleep-on-in-fantasy-drafts-040000766.html)
[PFF WR sleepers](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-sleeper-wide-receivers-2026)
[Seahawks role preview](https://www.seahawks.com/news/top-2026-training-camp-storylines-does-rashid-shaheed-still-handle-both-return-jobs)

**Why he fits:** Shaheed's speed and downfield role directly access the 40- and
50-yard TD bonuses. His 2025 Seattle receiving line was modest, so the thesis must
be a larger offensive role—not last year's production.

**Important scoring correction:** Return yardage is worth only 0.1 points per 25
yards in this league. Do not raise Shaheed because he is an elite returner; nearly
all of his sleeper value must come as a receiver.

**Failure case:** He remains behind Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Cooper Kupp, and/or Tory
Horton for consistent targets. Expect volatile weekly output even if the pick hits.

### 5. Jared Goff — best custom-scoring QB sleeper

**Why coverage is building:** Yahoo listed Goff among its QB targets, and
FantasyPros' August 17 QB sleeper consensus placed him fourth. FantasyPros also
noted four straight seasons with at least 4,400 passing yards and 29 TDs while he
was being drafted outside the top 15 quarterbacks.
[Yahoo targets and fades](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/fantasy-football-draft-targets-and-fades-scott-pianowski-reveals-his-top-picks-and-players-to-avoid-at-each-position-184335459.html)
[FantasyPros QB sleeper consensus](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/rankings/qb-sleepers.php)
[FantasyPros value cases](https://www.fantasypros.com/2026/07/fantasy-football-value-picks-by-ecr-2026/)

**Why he fits:** Passing yards score 25% more than the common four-points-per-100
yards baseline, and 300-/400-yard games earn bonuses. Goff's limited rushing lowers
his appeal in ordinary formats but matters less here when he reaches passing
milestones.

**Failure case:** A one-QB league still has a deep replacement pool, so taking him
before Round 9 creates unnecessary opportunity cost. He supplies a stable
late-QB path rather than a league-winning rushing ceiling.

### Quarterback

#### Malik Willis — ceiling bet, not the default one-QB plan

Willis ranked third in FantasyPros' August 17 QB sleeper poll and was included by
Yahoo and CBS. His rushing usage creates spike-week potential even if Miami's pass
catchers and passing efficiency disappoint.
[FantasyPros QB sleepers](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/rankings/qb-sleepers.php)
[FantasyPros Willis and Okonkwo cases](https://www.fantasypros.com/2026/07/10-fantasy-football-sleepers-that-will-win-your-draft-2026/amp/)
[CBS sleepers](https://secure-www.cbssports.com/fantasy/football/news/dave-richards-sleepers-1-0-mahomes-could-be-the-steal-of-your-draft/)

The league's five-point rushing TDs trim one point from the conventional rushing-QB
advantage, while enhanced passing-yard scoring favors Goff. Draft Willis only if
waiting beyond the intended QB tier, pairing two late QBs, or playing Superflex.

#### Dalton Kincaid — upside TE with a health/usage warning

NFL.com recommends Kincaid in Round 11 or later and cites elite yards-per-route
efficiency, but FantasyPros notes recurring knee and soft-tissue issues plus a
possible managed workload. The local injury feed currently projects no missed
games, which is not the same as proving a full snap share.
[NFL.com Kincaid case](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-fantasy-football-six-late-round-sleepers-to-target)
[FantasyPros Kincaid risk and upside](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/trade/chigoziem-okonkwo-dalton-kincaid.php?scoring=STD)

Kincaid is the higher-ceiling TE choice; Okonkwo is the cheaper price/role choice.
Do not draft both unless the bench is deep enough to carry two tight ends.

### Running back

#### Chris Rodriguez Jr. — workload value with a scoring mismatch

NFL.com recommends Rodriguez in Round 10 after citing similar camp reps to
Bhayshul Tuten and strong 2025 rushing-efficiency measures. FantasyPros separately
identified Jacksonville's 250-plus vacated touches as the backfield opportunity.
[NFL.com Rodriguez case](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-fantasy-football-six-late-round-sleepers-to-target)
[FantasyPros late RB targets](https://www.fantasypros.com/2026/07/4-late-round-running-backs-to-target-2026-fantasy-football/)

He is a good price-versus-workload bet, but the expected red-zone role is less
valuable here because rushing TDs are worth five instead of six. Prefer Coleman if
both are equally cheap and you want all-purpose ceiling; prefer Rodriguez when you
need the clearer immediate workload.

#### Kenneth Gainwell — floor plus contingent upside

Yahoo and FantasyPros have highlighted Gainwell after a 2025 receiving breakout
and a move to Tampa Bay. FantasyPros' current note says he averaged 13.3 touches and
85 total yards from Weeks 11-18 last season.
[Yahoo early sleepers](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/early-fantasy-football-sleepers-9-162018365.html)
[FantasyPros Gainwell profile](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/kenneth-gainwell.php)

His pass-catching raises the floor, but half-PPR reduces the benefit relative to
the full-PPR articles recommending him. His best outcome requires either a larger
standalone role or missed time from Bucky Irving.

### Important limitations

- ADP differs materially by platform; Stribling is the clearest current example.
- Many sleeper articles use full PPR, while this league uses half-PPR.
- Training-camp praise and preseason production are weaker than regular-season
  routes, targets, and high-value touches.
- The league's roster size, bench depth, draft slot, playoff weeks, and bonus
  stacking remain unconfirmed.
- Current health reports can change after a single practice.
- Suggested rounds are conservative heuristics based on the August 21 market,
  not a fitted projection model or guarantee of player performance.
